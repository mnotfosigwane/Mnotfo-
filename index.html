📘 Biology Notes (High School)

1. Cell Structure & Organization

Cells are the basic units of life. All living organisms are made of cells.

  • Nucleus: Controls cell activities and contains DNA
  • Mitochondria: Site of aerobic respiration
  • Ribosomes: Protein synthesis
  • Cell membrane: Controls movement in and out
  • Cell wall: Support in plant cells

2. Nutrition in Plants

Plants make their own food through photosynthesis.

Word equation: Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

Factors affecting photosynthesis: Light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, temperature.

3. Nutrition in Humans

Humans need a balanced diet for growth and energy.

  • Carbohydrates – energy
  • Proteins – growth and repair
  • Fats – energy storage
  • Vitamins & minerals – body regulation

4. Respiration

Respiration releases energy from food.

Aerobic respiration: Glucose + Oxygen → Energy + CO₂ + Water

Anaerobic respiration: Releases less energy without oxygen.

5. Transport in Humans

The circulatory system transports oxygen, nutrients, and waste.

  • Heart – pumps blood
  • Arteries – carry blood away
  • Veins – carry blood to the heart
  • Capillaries – exchange substances

6. Excretion

Excretion is the removal of toxic waste products.

Main excretory organs: kidneys, lungs, skin.

7. Coordination & Response

The nervous system helps organisms respond to stimuli.

Stimulus → Receptor → Coordinator → Effector → Response

8. Reproduction

Reproduction ensures continuation of species.

  • Asexual reproduction – one parent
  • Sexual reproduction – two parents

9. Ecology

Ecology studies interactions between organisms and their environment.

  • Food chains and food webs
  • Energy flow
  • Conservation of resources
